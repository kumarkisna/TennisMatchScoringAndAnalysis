## Rule
Keep only the two newest match records on Home. Put older records on a distinct History screen, sorted newest first.

**Why:** The user said History should not reuse the Home screen and wants the main screen kept uncluttered.

**How to apply:** Route the History bottom tab to its own screen and derive the archive from the date-sorted match list; keep the Home tab free of a match-count badge.

**Rule:** Opponent and result filters belong on the full History archive, alongside the completed-match toggle; do not constrain Home's two newest matches with archive filters.

**Why:** Search is useful for finding older meetings, while filtering only Home's two-card subset could imply that no matching older record exists.

**How to apply:** Filter the already-loaded on-device summaries on History, preserve newest-first order, and leave saved match records and Home layout unchanged.
