# TennisScoring — “Every point, remembered”

## Director’s treatment
- **Promise:** A match is more than a final score. TennisScoring helps a player or coach capture the point-by-point story and revisit it later, all on the device.
- **Identity:** Courtside editorial: deep evergreen, warm chalk, fluorescent tennis-ball citron, and a single clay-orange counterpoint. Condensed, athletic display lettering against crisp utility sans and scorekeeping mono.
- **Hero:** A luminous, believable Android scorebook rendered as tactile motion-graphic interface panels; court lines, score numerals, and point tokens become transition carriers.
- **Motion:** Scoreboard numerals snap and roll; court geometry becomes UI dividers; controlled camera push-throughs connect phone-sized views to full-frame detail.
- **Arc:** Open with a point becoming a permanent record; move from player setup into live scoring and rich details; widen into development and safe local backups.
- **Payoff:** The busy scorebook folds into a single calm match record and returns to the opening tennis-ball orbit, making the loop feel intentional.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
